A one-line, 1-star review written by Gary the monstera about his human owner: 63 days without water, then a flood so violent his roots filed for divorce.

`main.py` writes the review to `/output/entry.txt`, with a self-check that it is a single line of 1–279 characters. The line was picked by duel-testing 7 original drafts pairwise against the judge brief — it won all 6 of its duels.
